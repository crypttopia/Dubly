# Dubly Terms of Use

Last updated: September 27, 2026. These terms describe Dubly version 0.9.27.

## Service and API key

Dubly is a free Chrome extension for live AI dubbing of audio from a browser tab you select and optional Voice Typing. You supply your own valid Google Gemini API key. Google determines model availability, supported regions, quotas, and any charges for your account. Free use of Dubly does not mean unlimited or permanently free access to Gemini API.

## Starting and stopping a session

By pressing Start, you instruct Dubly to capture audio from the active tab and send it directly to Google Gemini API for processing with your key. This applies to that tab and session. Closing the extension popup alone does not stop transmission. Press Stop or close the source tab to end it. Floating subtitles, if selected, display translated text on the selected tab for video, audio-only players, or live audio without playing dubbed audio and use the same Gemini connection.

Voice Typing website access is requested only after you choose to enable it and is used to display the floating control and insert text into the active editable field. Microphone transmission begins only after you press Start on that control. Microphone audio and your key go directly to Google Gemini Transcribe Live; returned text remains in the local control until inserted, copied, deleted, or closed. These items do not pass through or get stored on Dubly servers.

## Your responsibilities

Use Dubly only with content you are authorized to process and transmit. Respect copyright, the source website's rules, Google's terms, regional and age restrictions, and any API quota or charges. Keep your API key private. Do not use Dubly for unlawful activity.

## Availability and accuracy

Translations can be inaccurate, incomplete, or delayed. Do not rely on them alone for medical, legal, financial, or other sensitive decisions. Preview models and Google service access may change. Dubly may briefly pause the active player once to compensate for initial delay, but does not guarantee support for every website or player and does not provide lip synchronization or music separation.
